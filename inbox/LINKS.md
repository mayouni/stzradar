# Links for the desk

Drop a link here, or give it to the desk in a session. One line each: the day, the link, and why it caught your eye.
The desk turns each line into an entry, or marks it `declined:` with the reason, and writes the entry's id at the end
of the line.

    2026-11-08  <link>  <why it matters>   -> <entry id | declined: reason>
