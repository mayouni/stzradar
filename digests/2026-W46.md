# Week 2026-W46 (November 9 to 15, 2026)

Twenty developments were kept this week, each read at its source and judged against what Softanza builds. Most came
from links the author sent; four came from a reading of NVIDIA's releases. One thread runs through the week: small
models on one machine. Which one answers, how fast it is ready, how several share one memory and one GPU, and how a
machine shows another which model it runs. Nine of the twenty proposals were built and measured in Softanza within
the week, and their entries carry the figures.

## Screens and agents

**A2UI 1.0: an agent answers with a native screen** (release, advice). The protocol sends a screen as data built from
a locked catalog of components. Softanza's Zane adds intent and law above it. A measurement is done: under
constrained decoding a 1B model wrote a valid A2UI screen 6 times of 12 in 222 tokens, and a valid Zane declaration
5 times in 69 tokens. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-a2ui-1-0.md)

**NVIDIA OpenShell governs an agent from outside its process** (release, advice). A supervisor outside the agent
checks every outbound request against a deny-by-default policy. Softanza decides its rules in code and adds that
outer boundary. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-nvidia-openshell.md)

**NVIDIA SkillSpector scans an agent skill before it is installed** (release, advice). 71 patterns in 17 categories.
Softanza checks its own agent files the same way. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-12-nvidia-skillspector.md)

## Which small model, and when to call a larger one

**NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B: a 4 GB model for local agents** (release, advice). Post-trained from Gemma 4 for
tool use. Softanza adds the Gemma 4 family to its decoder and takes the model to its court.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-nemotron-bolt-e4b.md)

**Liquid AI's LFM3: six 2B-class models on one backbone** (release, advice, done). Softanza learns from its two
decision models, which answer a fixed question in a fixed shape. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-liquid-lfm3.md)

**Rayline's ARC picks the model for each step of an agent** (release, advice, done). A 0.8B router predicts success
and cost per candidate. Softanza measured its own local-first escalation.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-12-rayline-arc.md)

**Samsung's LittleBit stores weights in under one bit each** (paper, advice, done). Softanza weighed memory against
quality on its small models. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-12-samsung-littlebit.md)

## The engine under the models

**llama.cpp and ggml reach version 1.0.0** (release, advice). Both now promise semantic versioning. Softanza moves
its engine's ggml to the stable line. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-13-llama-cpp-v1.md)

**Speculative decoding with a small drafter** (practice, advice). NVIDIA ships a drafter with each new model.
Softanza built the algorithm in its own decoder: the text is identical, and on a processor it measured 0.72 to 0.97
times the plain speed. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-speculative-decoding-dflash.md)

**Tur decodes eight sequences in one pass** (release, advice). 123 tokens per second for one sequence, 729 for
eight, because the weights are read once per step. Softanza measures the same rule on its decoder.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-15-tur-metal-inference.md)

## Memory: what stays ready

**NVIDIA Snapshot restores a warm inference worker in seconds** (release, advice, done). Softanza saves the warm
state it pays for, a read prompt: six answers went from 29.9 seconds to 4.0, and a prompt returns from disk in 113 ms.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-11-nvidia-snapshot.md)

**Google's TPU Sync treats chip, host memory, disk and network as one hierarchy** (release, advice, done). Softanza
built the same idea at the size of one machine: up to 32 models in named slots under one memory budget. A held model
gives its first token in about 75 ms; one read back from disk takes 1.1 to 2.7 seconds.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-14-google-tpu-sync.md)

## One GPU, several jobs

**OpenInfer: a serving GPU's math units are busy 6 to 11% of the time** (paper, advice). Softanza built the first
rule of sharing: one process owns the GPU at a time, and a second one waits or runs on the processor.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-14-openinfer-gpu-os.md)

**GPUOS: background work that yields at checkpoints** (paper, advice, done). Softanza measured it on its own card: a
foreground step of 1.5 ms waits about 100 ms behind a background job that never yields, and 4.6 ms behind one that
submits slices of about 7 ms, while that job keeps 96% of its rate.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-15-gpuos-shared-gpu.md)

**llmgw: one gateway in front of an organisation's own GPUs** (release, advice). Softanza learns its priority bands,
a person's call ahead of background agents, and its residency rule.
[Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-14-llmgw-gateway.md)

## Trust between machines

**NVIDIA FLARE 2.9.1 admits a site only after its hardware and software match an approved list** (release, advice,
done). Softanza built the admission rule for its compute federation: a host with one changed model byte is refused at
joining, in 0.76 ms. [Read the entry](../items/2026/2026-11-14-nvidia-flare-confidential.md)
