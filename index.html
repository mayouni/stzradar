How to read Lookout Radar

What it is
Every week Lookout reads what changed in AI at its source, and places each piece of news on the Softanza estate: the machine, the language, the foundation and every project built on them.
Reading as
Say who you are once. Each signal then speaks to you in one sentence, and its page opens with what you need first: checks to run for a developer, the work proposed for a maintainer, a lesson for a learner.
This week
The week's signals on a radar: each slice is a layer of the estate, and the closer a dot sits to the centre, the more it matters.
The estate
The whole Softanza stack, layer by layer, and a grid of domains against layers: click any number to see those signals.
All signals
Every live signal, filtered by project, layer, domain, concern or level.
Lookout bar
One line of these notices on your own site, from one script tag.
Levels
to know, to consider, to do, to do now.
