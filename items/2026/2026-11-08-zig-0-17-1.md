## What happened

The Zig project released 0.17.1, a bug-fix release over 0.17.0. Its announcement says it addresses more than 57
issues reported after 0.17.0, with a focus on regressions and on what stopped people from upgrading, and that most
users who could not upgrade to 0.17.0 should now be able to. Neither page lists a breaking change of its own; the
release notes linked are those of 0.17.0. The news page dates the release November 3, 2026 and the download page
November 2.

## Why it matters to Softanza

Softanza's engine is written in Zig, and so is much of Haro. On 2026-11-08 the toolchain installed for building them
is 0.15.2, which is also the minimum version one of the repositories declares. 0.17.1 is therefore two minor
versions ahead, each of which may change the language and the standard library.

That is why the stance is to watch and measure, not to adopt. A Zig minor version is not a patch, and the way to
learn its cost is one build on a branch, with the parallelism capped as this estate's machines require, counted
before anybody plans the move.

## What we do

- Propose to stzlib, then to Haro, one at a time, a measured trial build on 0.17.1.
- Look again by 2026-12-08, with the measurements if they exist.
